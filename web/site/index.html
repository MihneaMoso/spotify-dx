Spotify DX

A Spotify client that feels like open.spotify.com. Free, open-source and cross-platform for desktop, mobile and web.

Open Spotify DX

Sign in the way the web does

The app opens the real open.spotify.com login in-app — password, 2FA and passkeys all work. Your session persists, so you stay logged in across restarts.

Playback for everyone

Premium accounts use the official Web Playback SDK. Free accounts get an open multi-source engine — full tracks, not just 30-second previews.

Ad-free listening

An in-process AdGuard blocklist engine drops third-party ad and tracker requests before they hit the network.
